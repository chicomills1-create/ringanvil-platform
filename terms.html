Pilot Terms

RingAnvil is a brand of Apex Grid Engineering PLLC. These are the plain-language pilot terms for founding customers. A signed pilot agreement controls if anything here differs. Last updated: November 2, 2026.

What RingAnvil is

An automated answering and lead-follow-up service for trades businesses. RingAnvil answers calls in your business name, captures and qualifies leads against your rules, and gathers a caller's preferred appointment window for your team to review. Its pilot workflow is designed to prepare a structured call summary of every interaction. Automatic delivery to your team will be verified as part of pilot activation. RingAnvil is a receptionist, never a technician: it gives no diagnosis beyond your approved triage script and quotes no prices beyond rates you have approved in writing.

Disclosure — on every call

Every call opens with a recorded-call disclosure. The assistant does not claim to be a human employee, and identifies itself as an automated assistant when asked. Callers' data is handled under our Privacy Policy.

Emergencies

RingAnvil's pilot workflow is designed to identify urgent service requests and follow your approved escalation instructions. Live call transfers require configuration and verification before activation. For immediate danger or life-threatening emergencies, callers must contact 911. RingAnvil is not an emergency or dispatch service.

Follow-up messaging

Follow-up calling and messaging are planned for configured pilot workflows, subject to applicable consent and opt-out requirements. No automated outbound follow-up is active today. When follow-up runs, calls and texts go only to people who contacted your business first, and STOP and opt-out requests are honoured by design — instantly and permanently — with quiet hours respected in the recipient's timezone. RingAnvil never places cold outbound calls.

Refunds

We want to be fair, and we want to be clear. Refunds are for our failure to deliver what you paid for — not for service you received and used.

1. What is refundable

Refunds apply only to the portion of service we failed to deliver. Work we have performed, setup work we have begun, and service you have used are earned and are not refundable.

If we fail to deliver a material part of the service, we will first try to fix it. If we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, we may issue a service credit. A refund is the last step, and it covers only the undelivered portion — not the full subscription or setup fee.

Overage minutes are billed based on actual call handling and are not refundable unless a direct, provable failure in our handling caused the overage. Services are delivered according to the latest configuration as of the timestamp recorded in your account.

2. Subscriptions

Subscription fees are non-refundable for the current billing period, including if you cancel partway through the period. Your service continues through the end of the period you have paid for, unless we terminate it for cause under these Terms.

If we fail to deliver the service for a material portion of the period, the remedy in Section 1 applies to the undelivered portion only.

3. Setup fees

Setup fees are earned when setup work begins and are non-refundable. This includes configuration, number provisioning, call-flow setup, voicemail and forwarding setup, staff training, and any other onboarding work we have started, even if you cancel before your service goes live.

Setup fees are refundable only if we fail to begin setup work at all.

4. Failure to deliver

A failure to deliver means a material failure by RingAnvil to provide the answering service as described — for example, a sustained outage or a failure to answer calls during your contracted coverage hours.

Calls were answered materially wrong if logs and recordings show the wrong business name, incorrect script usage, or misapplication of emergency-routing rules per your account configuration.

A failure does not include:

  • a single missed call;
  • an isolated incident that does not materially affect your coverage;
  • calls outside your contracted hours;
  • calls you or your team answered, declined, or failed to route to us;
  • caller behavior outside our control; or
  • your own equipment, phone carrier, or internet failure.

For any failure claim, you must notify us promptly and give us a reasonable opportunity to cure it. A refund is not available for a failure we were not given the chance to fix, except where the failure is so complete that cure is not possible.

5. Chargebacks and disputes

If you file a chargeback, we will pause only the specific service and billing item in dispute while the chargeback is resolved. Other services and obligations continue.

We will respond to the chargeback with a full evidence pack, including your agreement, service records, call logs, and communications.

Filing a chargeback does not cancel your account, erase amounts owed for undisputed service, or entitle you to a refund for service you have used.

6. Abuse and future service

We may decline future service only where we have documented fraud, misuse of the service, or repeated abusive disputes. A single good-faith dispute, by itself, is not a basis to decline future service.

Your number

Your existing number is ported or forwarded only on your written instruction. Outbound calls present your business's own number or a number properly assigned to it — never a spoofed caller ID.

Cancellation and your data

On cancellation we stop answering within one business hour of instruction, and you keep your exported lead data. Recordings are handled per the Privacy Policy.
